WORLD: atoll
WORLD_PITCH: A coral island seen from the deep: arches, tide pools, a wreck and a whirlpool, each level teaching one trick the sea plays on the ball.

LEVEL 1: the arch
IDEA: A hollow sea arch: the ball slips under the crown from the open bottom and clears it from the inside, so the player only has to keep the ball alive and learn the angles.
